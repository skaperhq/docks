# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and respectful community. Participants
must treat others with empathy and professionalism regardless of experience,
identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Give constructive feedback and assume good intent.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and differing perspectives.
- Focus disagreement on ideas and technical outcomes.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when appropriate.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical, with decisions based on context, impact, and community safety.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
